Mohamed, owner

A family in the car business since 1952

It started in 1952 with Mohamed's late father and his auto repair workshop in Abdeen, Cairo. Mohamed carried the trade forward: from 1993 in Kuwait at the official dealers of Nissan & Infiniti, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai and Toyota & Lexus, and as a mechanics instructor; then from 2008 in Canada as a Red Seal licensed technician with Nissan, Hyundai, Dodge, Mitsubishi, Honda and Volkswagen, and as a vehicle inspector at Manheim and ADESA. M&M Auto Center has been his shop in Mississauga since 2021.

Common questions

How do I get a price?

Prices depend on your vehicle and the part. Send us your request in the chat or call us, and we confirm price and availability with you. We do not post prices online.

What do you need to give me a quote?

Your vehicle year, make and model, or the 17-character VIN, and a short description of what you need.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Book online button, or start the chat and choose Book online.

Do you offer pre-purchase inspections?

Yes. Tell us the car you are thinking of buying and we inspect it before you buy.

Which languages do you speak?

English and Arabic. The whole website and the chat are available in both.

When are you open?

Monday to Thursday 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Friday 9:30 AM to 12:00 PM and 2:00 to 6:00 PM, with a break for Friday prayer. Saturday 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM. Closed Sunday. The chat takes your request at any hour and we reply when we open.
